Assemble the mascot

In this interactive activity you handle a segmented 3D model. Tap the mascot to explode it into parts, then drag each piece back into place — it snaps when it gets close. Hold a piece to open details about it.

Interactive activity 3D model · Mascot

What to do

  • 👆 Tap the mascot to explode it into parts.
  • 🧩 Drag each piece back — it snaps when it gets close.
  • ⏱️ Hold a piece for 2s to open its details.
  • 🔄 Use Explode / Assemble to reset.
